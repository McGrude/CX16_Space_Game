## HYG star catalog

`universe_builder/data/source/hygdata_v42.csv` is the HYG v4.2 catalog. The original repository's phase 0 documentation identifies its source as David Nash / Astronomy Nexus, https://astronexus.com/projects/hyg, and its license as Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The catalog is preserved unchanged in this reorganization. Generated star catalogs/maps transform it by selecting nearby stars, projecting coordinates, resolving collisions, and adding synthetic names. Retain source attribution and applicable catalog license information with distributed catalog-derived data; do not label it as MIT-licensed project code. Verification of bundled upstream notices and exact dataset provenance is included in the next validation milestone.

## Local emulator materials

The ignored `EMU/` directory may contain an emulator, ROM, symbols, manuals, and disk images. These are local development materials, are not tracked as part of this repository, and are not covered by the project's MIT grant. Preserve their upstream notices if packaging them separately.
